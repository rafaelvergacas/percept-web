5 · Reporting and enforcement

You can block any user from their profile. A blocked user cannot message you, and you stop seeing them.

You can report any message or any profile from inside the app. Reports go to a person who reads them. Tell us what happened — the more specific you are, the faster it gets resolved.

Where a report is justified, or where we otherwise become aware of a breach, we may:

  • remove the content;
  • hide or deactivate a chat;
  • permanently delete the account.

Whenever we remove your content or delete your account, we will email you to say what we did and why — which rule was breached, the content concerned, and how to challenge it. Those decisions are made by a person, not automatically.

We act in proportion to what happened and how often. Serious breaches — threats, anything involving minors, sexual content sent to someone who did not ask for it — skip straight to permanent removal.

If you believe your account was removed in error, write to support@percep2.com and we will review it.

Reporting abuse to us is not a substitute for reporting a crime. If someone is in danger, contact the police.

7 · Messages expire

Percep2 deletes messages on a schedule: 7 days in Open Chats, 30 days in Closed Chats and private conversations. Open Chats themselves expire two hours after they are created unless converted to a Closed Chat.

This is how the product is meant to work. Percep2 is not a storage service and not a backup. Do not use it to keep anything you would be upset to lose, and do not expect to be able to retrieve a conversation later.
